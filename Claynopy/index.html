This is a front-end simulation only — a browser can't read your device's real screen-time data (that lives behind Android's Usage Access permission or iOS Screen Time). Drag "used today" on each app to stand in for that data. Every 15 minutes past an app's limit costs one tree, pulled from the same shared pool of 100, and deaths are permanent for the day — they don't come back until tomorrow's reset.
